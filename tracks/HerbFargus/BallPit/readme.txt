Ball Pit (BallPit.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A giant ball pit to drive around in. Two hundred 10 m balls in twelve
colours fill a round arena 360 m across, on a padded soft-play floor that
banks up at the edge, walled in by three huge inflatable rings. An invisible
ceiling keeps everything inside, so balls you knock flying come back down.
Shove through them, bounce them off the rings, or clear a path with the horn
ball.

Race it solo: set opponents to 0. With AI cars in the pit the game has
crashed after a while -- an AI car can get crushed in the pile-up of balls.
Solo is fine.

Install: put BallPit.tra and BallPit.stp in the game folder and install it
into a track slot with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan). The
name shows in full in any slot whose name is at least 7 letters long --
every slot except Dayton and Dundas.

Every mesh is under 1,000 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the geometry, collision,
racing lines, textures, sky and track map. No game or third-party art.
