Cats vs Dogs (CatsVsDogs.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A 1.5 km lap split down the middle by a fence: dog country on one side, cat
country on the other. You cross into dog country at the start gate and back
into cat country through a giant cat flap.

Dog country: a frisbee kicker over a 38 m gap, a hairpin past a row of
doghouses, a slalom through giant fire hydrants, and Fetch Lane, a straight
that ends in six dogs set up like bowling pins. A bone-shaped pond sits in
the infield -- it's real water, so drive in and you'll float.

Cat country: a tunnel of cardboard boxes, a bend past the fish market, a
rooftop run that climbs onto the roofs and drops off the edge onto a landing
ramp, and a hairpin round a giant scratching-post tower. A giant ball of yarn
sits in the infield.

Thirty-six cats and dogs, four times life size, wait round the lap. Each one
is a 50 lb obstacle, so you can knock them flying.

Made for the Alley Cat and Doghouse cars.

Install: put CatsVsDogs.tra and CatsVsDogs.stp in the game folder and install
it into a track slot with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan).
The name shows in full in any slot whose name is at least 10 letters long:
Silverdale, Rock Island, Ridge Valley, Castlegreen or Sunset Mesa.

Every mesh is under 1,000 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the geometry, collision,
racing lines, textures, sky and track map. No game or third-party art.
