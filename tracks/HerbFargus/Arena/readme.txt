Arena (Arena.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

"Continuous Fire", the Arena Death Match map from Streets of SimCity
(Maxis, 1997), rebuilt at true SoSC scale as a Viper Racing track. A
2.9 km lap runs round the ring road on top of the arena's berm, past the
rows of 44 m towers. Inside, the floor drops away: a paved plaza with four
sloped blocks, and a herd of cows.

  - 100 cows in two herds, one in the centre plaza and one in the ring
    between the plaza and the berm. 90 of them are loose 50 lb cows that
    roll away when you hit them (they come back for the next race); 10 are
    rooted to the spot.
  - A 60 m cow statue in the middle of the plaza. Its belly is 23 m up, so
    you can drive underneath it; only its hooves are solid.
  - The towers are solid on every side.

The racing line stays on the ring road. Everything else -- the floor, the
blocks, the berm faces -- can be driven, but the arena keeps SoSC's steep
heights, and a car that takes them at speed will take damage. Turn damage
off in the game's options if you want to play in the arena rather than
race round it.

Install: put Arena.tra and Arena.stp in the game folder and install it
into a track slot with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan).
The name is short enough to show in full in any slot. The game's drivers.res must be the
empty one (vrmod's Doctor offers it), or the AI follow the original
track's racing line instead of this one.

Tested on the viperport engine with a full 9-car field. Uses about 106 of
the engine's 512 physics objects, nearly all of them cows. Loose objects
and AI cars have crashed the game on other tracks in big pile-ups; if that
happens here, race it solo.

From Streets of SimCity (c) 1997 Maxis / Electronic Arts: the arena itself
(the Continuous Fire scenario's city, Arena.sc2), its ground, road and
building textures, the towers, trees and the cow. Converted with vrmod;
the collision, racing line, statue and cow physics are new.
