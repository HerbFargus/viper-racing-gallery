Burninator (Burninator.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

Climb the flank of a volcano, jump the lava-filled crater off its rim, and
careen down the far side, then loop home through the jungle. A burninating
dragon stands on the crater rim and breathes a fireball across the jump, and
you fly straight through it. On the far rim, monks with their heads on fire
hop about in front of a burning cottage. They're knockable: see if you can
get one into the lava. More cottages burn in the jungle, with their own
hopping monks, and ten glowing magma boulders roll down the volcano at the
start and flatten anything in their way.

The jump: there's no ramp, the crater rim is the lip, and the crater is 60 m
across, so you need about 77 mph at the top. Fall short and you land in the
lava. It floats your car but gives it no grip. Press space to reset and
you're put back on the rim beside the dragon (or use the wheelie hack to fly
out). AI cars that fall in stay there.

Race it with AI or solo. Tested with 7 AI opponents.

Install: put Burninator.tra and Burninator.stp in the game folder and install
it into a track slot with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan).
The name shows in full in any slot whose name is at least 10 letters long:
Ridge Valley, Castlegreen, Rock Island, Sunset Mesa or Silverdale. It was
built and tested in the Sunset Mesa slot.

Every mesh is under 1,000 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the geometry, collision,
racing lines, textures, sky and track map. No game or third-party art. The
dragon is a homage to Trogdor the Burninator.
