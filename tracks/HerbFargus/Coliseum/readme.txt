Coliseum (Coliseum.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A banked oval inside a giant stadium dome, open to a starry night sky. The
road is sized like Bemidji's -- 28 m wide, with its 16.7-degree banked
turns -- but it's a plain oval, 2.2 km a lap. Raked stands of empty seats
rise all the way round to the dome wall, trimmed in neon, and the whole
infield is a glowing cyan floor.

In the middle of the infield two ramps face each other across a 115 m gap.
Hit either one at about 100 mph and you'll clear the gap and land on the far
ramp's downslope; it works in both directions. Any slower and you'll come up
short. Sixty giant coloured balls drop into the arena at the start: shove
them around, or use the horn ball to clear them out of your way.

Everything inside the wall is drivable, so you can cut across the infield
or run up into the stands; anything that goes up the stands rolls back down.

Install: put Coliseum.tra and Coliseum.stp in the game folder and install it
into a track slot with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan). The
name shows in full in any slot whose name is at least 8 letters long:
Silverdale, Rock Island, Ridge Valley, Castlegreen or Sunset Mesa.

Every mesh is under 1,000 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the geometry, collision,
racing lines, textures, sky and track map. No game or third-party art.
