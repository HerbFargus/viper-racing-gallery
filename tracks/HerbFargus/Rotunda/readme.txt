Temple Rotunda (Rotunda.tra) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A round temple arena, 360 m across, lit by torches under a painted temple
sky. The floor is rings of stone tiles around a carved sun medallion, and its
outer 30 m bank up to the wall. There are columns to hide behind, eight
stone-head totems (all knockable), golden idols and a great idol in the
middle. Twenty hidden trap tiles on the flat floor launch any car that drives
over them. Look closely and you can pick them out.

Race it solo: set opponents to 0. It has only been tested without AI, and AI
cars launched by traps have crashed the game on other tracks.

Install: put Rotunda.tra in the game folder and install it into a track slot
with a track switcher (vrmod's Switcher, or TrackMan). It shows as "Rotunda"
in any slot whose name is at least 7 letters long.

Needs a build with a raised per-object vertex limit: a community 1.2.x build,
or vrmod's vertex-buffer patch. Its largest mesh is 1,460 vertices. Tested on
the v1.0 engine with vrmod's patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the geometry, collision,
racing lines, textures, sky and track map. No game or third-party art.
Try it with a boulder horn ball. The Willys Jeep and Indy Jeep in the
gallery carry one.
