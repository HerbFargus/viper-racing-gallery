J57 (j57.car, j57r.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

The J57 from Streets of SimCity (Maxis, 1997), the game's take on a 1966
Ford GT40, converted to drive in Viper Racing. Two versions:

  J57        j57.car    its own engine: 428 hp, 6,400 rpm redline
  J57 Race   j57r.car   Val's whole race file: 350 hp / 550 lb-ft,
                        9,000 rpm, six gears -- for racing the field

From Streets of SimCity (c) 1997 Maxis / Electronic Arts, unchanged in look:
  - the body and its textures (SIM3D2.MAX model STREETS_GT40, SIM3D.BMP)
  - the dashboard: SoSC's "race" cockpit panel (RACEF2), shown full width
  - the engine note: SoSC's Mustang V8 samples, the GT40's own Ford family
  - the horn ball: SoSC's missile, with its launch sound as the horn

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper chassis (Val in Moose
Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; his readme permits reusing its parts). The standard
version keeps the J57's own engine, sized to Val's weight; the Race version
takes his whole physics file.

Changes: converted with vrmod. Wheels fitted to the painted wheel arches,
brake lights placed on the painted tail lamps, every texel lifted off the
transparency key so the dark paint doesn't render see-through, and the
full model used at every distance.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Maxis or Electronic Arts. The art and
sounds are theirs; this is a fan conversion for people who own the game.
