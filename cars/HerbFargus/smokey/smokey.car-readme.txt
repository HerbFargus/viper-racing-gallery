Smokey (smokey.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

Model: an original low-poly model of the Smokey fire truck toy from the Putt-Putt
line, built by hand from photos of the toy: the red cab with its face (3D eyes, curved
brows, cheeks meeting at the nose, a toothy grin), the ladder, hoses and rear-view
mirrors that stick out.

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper (Val in Moose Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; his
readme permits reusing its parts), tuned for a tall body that rolls: 210 hp, 3.50 final
drive, 510/56 R22 tyres. The truck is 3.8 m long and 2.9 m tall.

Changes: converted to a Viper Racing car with vrmod. The engine (a V8), the siren horn
and the blue water-balloon horn ball are generated from scratch. The cockpit puts you in
the cab, looking out through the truck's face.

Install: copy smokey.car into the Viper Racing folder. It appears as "Smokey".
