Doghouse (doghouse.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A red plank doghouse bolted to a kart, with a corgi at the wheel looking out of
the door. Tennis balls for headlights, a bone for a bumper, a dog bowl and a
fire-hydrant exhaust on the back deck, a wagging tail for an antenna, and
paw-print hubcaps.

Press the horn and it barks, and throws a tennis ball.

Physics: Val's "best handling" Viper chassis (Val in Moose Jaw, 22 Oct 2014;
reuse permitted in his readme) with a 210 hp engine on tall gearing and
245/75 R16 tyres. It's tall, so it leans in the corners -- brake before the
hairpins.

The cockpit puts you inside the doghouse, looking out of the doorway over a
gauge board on the sill, with the tennis-ball lamps and the bone bumper out
front.

Made for the Cats vs Dogs track (CatsVsDogs.tra), with its partner car, the
Alley Cat.

Every mesh is under 2,200 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the model, textures,
cockpit, engine sound, horn and horn ball. No game or third-party art.
