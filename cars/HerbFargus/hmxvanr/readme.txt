HMX Utility Van (hmxvan.car, hmxvanr.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

The HMX Utility Van from Streets of SimCity (Maxis, 1997), the game's take
on a 1973 GMC C-Series van, converted to drive in Viper Racing. Two versions:

  HMX Utility Van        hmxvan.car    its own engine: 79 hp, 4,500 rpm
                                       redline. Authentically slow.
  HMX Utility Van Race   hmxvanr.car   Val's whole race file: 350 hp /
                                       550 lb-ft, 9,000 rpm, six gears

From Streets of SimCity (c) 1997 Maxis / Electronic Arts, unchanged in look:
  - the body and its textures (SIM3D2.MAX model STREETS_UTILITY, SIM3D.BMP)
  - the dashboard: SoSC's "utility" cockpit panel (UTILF3), shown full width
  - the engine note: SoSC's VW bus samples, the only commercial engine on
    the disc
  - the horn ball: SoSC's missile, with its launch sound as the horn

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper chassis (Val in Moose
Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; his readme permits reusing its parts). The standard
version keeps the van's own engine, sized to Val's weight; the Race version
takes his whole physics file.

Changes: converted with vrmod. The body is squeezed to Viper car length,
so the wheels are fitted to its painted arches rather than the real van's
wheelbase. Brake lights placed on the painted tail lamps, every texel
lifted off the transparency key so the dark paint doesn't render
see-through, and the full model used at every distance.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Maxis or Electronic Arts. The art and
sounds are theirs; this is a fan conversion for people who own the game.
