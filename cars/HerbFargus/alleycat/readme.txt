Alley Cat (alleycat.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

A chopped black hot rod with a cat for a face: pointed ears on the grille, slit
green headlights, whiskers, cat ears on the roof, and a curling tail for an
antenna. Fat rear tyres, skinny fronts, a chrome blower through the louvred
hood and purple flames down the sides. The paw-print hubcaps are on every wheel.

Press the horn and it meows, and throws a ball of yarn.

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper (Val in Moose Jaw, 22 Oct
2014; his readme permits reusing its parts) -- his whole physics file, with only
the hot rod's own dimensions and its 155/70 R15 front and 265/55 R17 rear tyres.

The cockpit is the car's own body seen from the driver's seat: a chopped-roof
slot windscreen, the blower and the grille's ears out front, with a generated
dash, gauges, name plate and steering wheel.

Made for the Cats vs Dogs track (CatsVsDogs.tra), with its partner car, the
Doghouse.

Every mesh is under 2,200 vertices. Tested on the v1.0 engine with vrmod's
vertex-buffer patch.

Everything in it is original, generated with vrmod: the model, textures,
cockpit, engine sound, horn and horn ball. No game or third-party art.
