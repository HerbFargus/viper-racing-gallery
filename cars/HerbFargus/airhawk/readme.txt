Airhawk (airhawk.car, airhawkr.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

The Airhawk from Streets of SimCity (Maxis, 1997), the game's gold-roofed
muscle car and its take on a 1969 Chevrolet Camaro, converted to drive in
Viper Racing. Two versions:

  Airhawk        airhawk.car    its own engine: 248 hp, 6,000 rpm redline
  Airhawk Race   airhawkr.car   Val's whole race file: 350 hp / 550 lb-ft,
                                9,000 rpm, six gears -- for racing the field

From Streets of SimCity (c) 1997 Maxis / Electronic Arts, unchanged in look:
  - the body and its textures (SIM3D2.MAX model STREETS_JAVELIN, SIM3D.BMP)
  - the dashboard: SoSC's "sedan" cockpit panel (SEDF2), shown full width
  - the engine note: SoSC's Camaro samples -- the actual car
  - the horn balls and horns: the Airhawk throws one of SoSC's cows and
    moos (cowhit.wav); the Airhawk Race lays one of its mines (minedep.wav)

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper chassis (Val in Moose
Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; his readme permits reusing its parts). The standard
version keeps the Airhawk's own engine, sized to Val's weight; the Race
version takes his whole physics file.

Changes: converted with vrmod. The Airhawk's source mesh was wound
inside-out and had to be turned the right way round; wheels fitted to the
painted wheel arches, brake lights placed on the painted tail lamps, every
texel lifted off the transparency key so the dark paint doesn't render
see-through, and the full model used at every distance.

Not affiliated with or endorsed by Maxis or Electronic Arts. The art and
sounds are theirs; this is a fan conversion for people who own the game.
