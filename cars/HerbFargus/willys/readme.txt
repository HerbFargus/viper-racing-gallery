Willys Jeep (willys.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

Model: "Willys Jeep Low Poly" by InnocentBaraka
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/willys-jeep-low-poly-57ab28eb0f21472ead1f5dadf4e73a27
  CC BY 4.0 -- http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Physics: Val's "best handling and performance" Viper (Val in Moose Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; his
readme permits reusing its parts) -- his whole physics file, with only the jeep's own
dimensions and 225/80 R16 tyres.

Changes: converted to a Viper Racing car with vrmod (wheels separated, scaled to its
tyres, brake lights added). Engine sound (a V10, for the Viper engine under it), the
boulder horn ball and its rumbling horn are generated from scratch. The cockpit is the
jeep's own body seen from the driver's seat, with a generated steering wheel, gauges and
dash plate fitted into it.
