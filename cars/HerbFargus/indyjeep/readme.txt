Indy Jeep (indyjeep.car) for Viper Racing -- by HerbFargus

Model: "Indianna Jones Adventure Jeep" by Kastle
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/indianna-jones-adventure-jeep-f4ceda2563df4b11a99f799bbb4c7d3d
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 -- this car is shared under the same licence: credit, non-commercial,
  share-alike. http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
  A fan model of the ride vehicle from Indiana Jones Adventure at Disneyland; not
  affiliated with or endorsed by Disney or Lucasfilm.

Physics: Val's "best handling" Viper chassis (Val in Moose Jaw, 22 Oct 2014; reuse
permitted in his readme) with a 270 hp straight-six and tall gearing.

Changes: converted with vrmod (wheels separated, the faces hidden between its blocks
removed, scaled to 315/75 R16 tyres, brake lights added). Its textures are new pixel
art in the original model's colours, and the engine sound, boulder horn ball and
rumbling horn are generated from scratch. The cockpit is the jeep's own body seen from
the driver's seat, with a generated steering wheel and gauge binnacle fitted into it.
